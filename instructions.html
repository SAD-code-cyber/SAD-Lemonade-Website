Instructions on how to make lemonade

For making yellow lemonade first you need to have:

Ingredients
Ingredient Quantity Side Notes
Lemons 5 Freshly squeezed
Sugar 1 cup Adjust to taste
Water 4 cups Filtered
Ice As needed Crushed or cubed
Mint leaves A handful For garnish

After gathering the ingredients you will then need to:

  1. Juice five lemons
  2. Heat one cup sugar until the sugar dissolves
  3. four cups of water
  4. In a pitcher,add ice, combine the lemon juice, cold water, and cooled simple syrup
  5. Now enjoy!
